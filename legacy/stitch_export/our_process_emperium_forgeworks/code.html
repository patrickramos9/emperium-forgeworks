The Ritual of Fabrication

Protocol 449-B: Quality Assurance

01 / INITIALIZATION

3D Printing

Utilizing high-density liquid resin and ultra-high-resolution monochromatic screens to capture every serrated edge and gothic detail.

02 / PURIFICATION

Chemical Wash

Submerged in specialized chemical baths to remove excess resin, ensuring the intricate scuplts remain sharp and free of residue.

03 / EXTRACTION

Support Removal

Meticulous removal of lattice structures. Each miniature is inspected under magnification for surgical precision.

04 / HARDENING

UV Curing

The final stage. Targeted UV radiation stabilizes the molecular structure, solidifying the resin into its permanent, rigid form.
